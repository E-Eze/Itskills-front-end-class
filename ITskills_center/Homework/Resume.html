About me

I’m interested in the mechanics of human cooperation—how people coordinate, commit, and build things together.

I have spent a lot of time consciously trying to understand how to “human” better. People are complex and unpredictable, but I noticed that in more structured environments, especially economic and organizational ones our behavior became legible. Contracts, incentives, roles, and norms made cooperation something you could actually study. I like to meet people.

My Work

Operations Lead — Archivi.ng (2020–2025)

Archivi.ng is a nonprofit organization working to make Nigerian history accessible to everyone, everywhere. I joined as one of the earliest employees and helped lay the foundation for its operational systems.

Key contributions include:

  • Designing and managing Archivi.ng Fellowship V1 through Luminate funding
  • Conducting historical research in support of Fear No Man a podcast documentary series produced by Higher Ground, the production company co-founded by Barack and Michelle Obama.
  • Writing archival essays, including a piece on the historical strength and political significance of Nigerian women.

Side quests & exploratory work

Alongside my core work, I pursued a few smaller, exploratory projects primarily as thinking tools rather than long-term initiatives:

Worlding a new browser

  • Conducted research exploring the browser as an interface for collective intelligence. Relying on the OHS (Open Hyperdocument System) by douglas Engelbart, Alan Kay and McLuhan’s Understanding Media.

Credit & Digital Identity Research
  • Early-stage research into credit infrastructure in Nigeria, later extending into questions of digital identity and migration verification. How we would help Africans bypass the growing anti-globalisation trend across the world. I ultimately stepped away after confronting the surveillance risks embedded in such systems.
Working Woman
  • An independent writing project where I research businesses and ideas and publish synthesized insights.

Faces of Web3 (2022–2023)

  • While on sabbatical from Archivi.ng, I began exploring Web3, initially as a way to understand the structural failure modes facing African artists and creators, and how new coordination tools might change them.

Through Faces of Web3, I worked across media, research, and community organizing:

DreamCatchers Academy
  • Facilitated a $500,000 grant from the AfroDroids community for DreamCatchers Academy. A school focused on transforming the lives of indigent and orphaned girls who have experienced neglect and homelessness.
Naija Flood Aid
  • Co-organized emergency relief during Nigeria’s 2022 floods, which displaced over a million people across Rivers, Bayelsa, and Plateau states following dam releases in Cameroon. Funds were deployed toward medical supplies, food, clothing, and temporary shelter.

This work led to my selection as a Mandela Washington Fellow.
